Kid Learning Portal – Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-25

Applies to: Kid Learning Portal for Android (io.github.truongngoclinh.klp), version 0.1.0 and later versions until this page is updated.

Short version

  • No sign-in. There are no accounts.
  • No personal data is collected.
  • Nothing is shared or sold.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking.

What stays on the device

The child's game progress is stored only on the device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent off the device.

Uninstalling the app removes it. A grown-up can also delete it at any time in the app's grown-ups area.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, or send:

  • names or email addresses
  • contacts or photos
  • location
  • microphone or audio recordings
  • camera images
  • device identifiers or advertising IDs (AD_ID)
  • crash logs
  • usage analytics

Third parties

The app has no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting.

Momo's voice uses the device's built-in text-to-speech. The app only gives it Momo's fixed lines, never anything about the child. If the device's text-to-speech engine uses an online voice, that is handled by the device's own speech service, not by this app.

Children

Kid Learning Portal is made for children ages 5 and under and 6–8 (Google Play Families).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone.

Before a grown-up deletes data in the app, the device asks them to confirm with Face ID, a fingerprint or the device PIN. That check happens on the device, and we receive nothing.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to location, the microphone, the camera, contacts or storage.

Changes

If a later version adds sign-in or sends any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

This web page

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits under its own privacy statement. The page itself uses no cookies and no scripts.
